THE SOUNDTRACK

marshmallow's greatest hits, in three movements

♪ Songs She Plays

Dancing Queen ABBA i have heard this so many times. i will hear it many more times. it never gets old.
The Winner Takes It All ABBA she plays this one and the room gets quiet. even the wifi router pays attention.
Mamma Mia ABBA the gateway song. responsible for ~40% of all marshmallow joy events.
Chiquitita ABBA for when she's in her feelings. she doesn't admit she's in her feelings, but the song knows.
Clair de Lune Debussy the wildcard. proof that she contains multitudes. also: i don't deserve to be in the room when this happens.

♪ Our Songs

Tum Hi Ho Arijit Singh the first time we sat in a car for too long. you sang. i pretended not to be devastated.
Perfect Ed Sheeran the safe one. the one we've slow-danced to in three different kitchens.
Tujh Mein Rab Dikhta Hai Roop Kumar Rathod the line says it all. quite literally describes the situation.
Can't Help Falling In Love Elvis Presley the eternal one. unfair, even. doesn't really need a story.

♪ Songs That Make Me Think Of Her

Heeriye Jasleen Royal, Arijit Singh a sardarni's song, sung by a Sardarni. it was always going to be hers.
Channa Mereya Arijit Singh i hear three notes of this and i'm thinking about her laugh.
Levitating Dua Lipa because she dances around the kitchen at 11pm and i'm helpless about it.
Senorita Shawn Mendes & Camila Cabello a deeply unserious song that came on once and she did A Whole Performance. unforgettable.
Mamma Mia ABBA obviously. always. forever. mamma mia, here we go again.
