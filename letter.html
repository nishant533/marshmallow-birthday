My dearest Marshmallow,

I should probably start with "happy birthday" — but you already know that part. The whole site you just clicked through is essentially a 6-page-long way of saying it. So let me say the rest.

You are, somehow, the most smallest and the most fiesty person I've ever known. You'll either sleep the entire day or have mad labrador energy (there is no in between). I love annoying you with my English pronunciations & crazy singing skillzz. You'll play "Dancing Queen" on the piano and forget how to navigate on google maps, both with equal dignity.

I love that you are loud where I am quiet. I love that you're brave in places I haven't worked up to yet. I love that you almost cried the first time our parents met and my mom ragged you. I love that you say "I told you so" in a way that should be illegal. I love that you've grown into this lovely, beatiful, every charming gal!

The thing nobody tells you about marrying someone from a different world (read: non engg.) is that you don't really meet in the middle. You build a whole new world together! I didn't know I wanted that until I had it. Until I had you.

You are 29 today. I have known you for some of those years and I am hoping to spend the rest with you (RIP long distance). I am going to spend them watching you become hotter every year.

Thank you for picking me. Thank you for letting me pick you. Thank you for being (not so) soft and sharp and silly and entirely, unmistakably yourself.

Happy birthday, My love. The rest of my life starts wherever you are (currently sitting next to you here in kondapur, sigh).

— Yours, always,
Your Extremely Handsome Boi ♡
