A LETTER

the real stuff

⚠ NOTE TO YOU (DELETE BEFORE MAY 12): This is my strawman draft. It's okay as-is, but it'll land 10x harder if you rewrite it in your own words. Keep what feels right, replace the rest with the real stuff — the actual moment you knew, the actual thing she says that makes you laugh, the actual reason this all feels right.

My dearest Marshmallow,

I should probably start with "happy birthday" — but you already know that part. The whole site you just clicked through is essentially a 6-page-long way of saying it. So let me say the rest.

You are, somehow, the most ridiculous and the most serious person I've ever known. You'll deadlift twice your body weight in the morning and cry at a Pixar trailer in the evening. You'll correct my Tamil pronunciation with the confidence of a woman who is a Sardarni from Punjab. You'll play "Dancing Queen" on the piano and forget how to fold a fitted sheet, both with equal dignity.

I love that you are loud where I am quiet. I love that you're brave in places I haven't worked up to yet. I love that you cried the first time my Amma made you sambar and then asked for the recipe. I love that you say "I told you so" in a way that should be illegal and isn't. I love that you've stood in 23 countries and somehow always come home.

The thing nobody tells you about marrying someone from a different world is that you don't really meet in the middle. You build a whole new world together — one with chai and filter coffee, one with bhangra at the wedding and Carnatic at the reception, one where the gods we grew up praying to learn to share a wall. I didn't know I wanted that until I had it. Until I had you.

You are 29 today. I have known you for some of those years and I am hoping to know you for many, many more. I am going to spend them learning the rest of "The Winner Takes It All" by ear so I can sit next to you on the piano bench and pretend I knew it all along. I am going to spend them watching you become whoever you decide to become next.

Thank you for picking me. Thank you for letting me pick you. Thank you for being soft and sharp and silly and entirely, unmistakably yourself.

Happy birthday, Marshmallow. The rest of my life starts wherever you are.

— Yours, always,
your Tamil Brahmin boy ♡
